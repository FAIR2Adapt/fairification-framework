# Metadata extraction and enrichment

The Knowledge Extraction and Metadata Enrichment services transform textual and document-based research resources into structured semantic metadata that can be incorporated into FAIR Digital Objects.

## Role in the FAIRification Framework

Climate-adaptation knowledge is distributed across scientific publications, reports and other textual resources. Important information is often present in the content but absent from structured metadata. The enrichment services analyse these resources and expose machine-actionable descriptions that can be combined with provenance and other FAIR metadata.

Outputs can include topic classifications, key elements and entities, including links to semantic resources where available.

## Response to user requirements

The CCA case studies require more than repository-level metadata. They need to discover, connect and reuse knowledge contained inside publications and grey literature.

### Turn unstructured resources into machine-actionable inputs

The services extract semantic information directly from textual resources. This supports FAIRification workflows in which the original publication remains part of the FAIR Digital Object while newly extracted metadata improves its interpretation and reuse.

### Support heterogeneous adaptation knowledge

The services accept several document and text formats and can therefore be incorporated into workflows involving scientific publications as well as other knowledge resources. This is important for case studies concerned with literature, adaptation platforms and knowledge translation.

### Align enrichment with stakeholder needs

The evaluation shows that semantic enrichment cannot be entirely generic. Custom tags and annotations need to evolve with stakeholder and community requirements. Refinement of these tags is therefore an explicit next step.

## Evaluation in the CCA case studies

Metadata enrichment is a core component of the **Knowledge Production from Scientific Publications** and **Literature Review and Extraction Pipeline** FAIR Solutions.

In the literature workflow, publications are selected, metadata are extracted, scientific claims are identified and validated, semantic information is added, and the resulting resource is packaged and published as a FAIR Digital Object. This demonstrates how enrichment can connect unstructured literature with machine-actionable FAIR resources.

Planned extensions include support for additional resource types such as policy documents and knowledge platforms, multilingual English/Portuguese resources and further alignment of annotations with stakeholder needs.

!!! info "Related services"
    Metadata enrichment is closely connected to **Scientific claims extraction and verification** and to I-ADOPT variable descriptions. Together they support the transition from human-readable documents to semantically enriched, machine-actionable FAIR Digital Objects.
