# FAIR Solutions

The FAIRification services developed within FAIR2Adapt are validated through **FAIR Solutions**: reusable blueprints for solving concrete FAIRification challenges in the Climate Change Adaptation (CCA) case studies.

Each FAIR Solution combines four elements:

1. a **demo Digital Object** representing the resource before FAIRification;
2. a **FAIRification Plan** derived from the identified requirements and FAIR gaps;
3. a **FAIRification Workflow** that applies the required FAIR Supporting Resources and services;
4. a **demo FAIR Digital Object (FDO)** representing the FAIRified result.

The FAIR Solutions therefore connect the user requirements with the operational FAIRification Framework and provide the main scenarios for evaluating the FAIR supporting services.

| FAIR Solution | Main scenario | Main FAIRification focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [#1 Data processing pipeline for coastal water quality modelling](solution-1-riomar.md) | RiOMar / coastal-ocean modelling | Large-scale, analysis-ready and cloud-optimised FAIR data |
| [#2 FAIR GIS workflow for urban climate risk assessment](solution-2-hamburg.md) | Hamburg urban flood risk | FAIR datasets, software, workflows, controlled access and transferability |
| [#3 Knowledge production from scientific publications](solution-3-knowledge.md) | Urban pluvial flood-risk knowledge | Machine-readable scientific statements linked to evidence and provenance |
| [#4 Literature review and extraction pipeline](solution-4-literature.md) | Portugal Adaptation Hub / climate literature | Metadata enrichment, claims, nanopublications and publication FDOs |

## Relationship with the service evaluation

The four FAIR Solutions are the practical validation layer of the Framework. They show that evaluation is not limited to testing individual services in isolation. Instead, the services are combined according to the needs of each case study and evaluated through their contribution to a complete FAIRification workflow.

See [Evaluation in the CCA case studies](../evaluation.md) for the cross-service view.
